SIC = seat-in-coach: you share the vehicle with other travelers. The operator allows a maximum 5-minute vehicle wait for guests, while guests may wait up to 45 minutes for shared vehicles. Be punctual and keep your phone available.

The voucher states airport waiting is up to 80 minutes after touchdown. Confirm the meeting time in light of the arrival-time discrepancy.
