MBK Center

Souvenirs · Fashion · Electronics

Keep shopping brief, then eat lunch at the MBK Center food court. MBK is shortened by one hour to preserve your hotel rest time. Leave straight after lunch, by 2:00 PM at the latest, and aim to be back at the Ramada by 3:00 PM for a relaxed afternoon before your dinner cruise.

2:00 PM: MBK → BTS → free hotel boat → Ramada
Leave MBK by 2:00 PM; target hotel arrival by 3:00 PM. Allow about an hour overall for walking, the train, and the hotel boat connection. This is a planning allowance; boat waiting times can extend the trip.
Step-by-step route back to the hotel
  1. From MBK Center, follow the skywalk signs to BTS National Stadium (W1).
  2. Buy a ticket to Saphan Taksin (S6) and board the Silom Line toward Bang Wa.
  3. Stay on the same train through Siam. No train change is needed. Get off at Saphan Taksin (S6).
  4. Follow signs to Sathorn/Taksin Pier.
  5. Find the Ramada Plaza Bangkok Menam Riverside hotel shuttle and take it back to the hotel. Confirm the correct boarding point with pier staff.

The BTS train is paid; the Ramada shuttle boat is free for hotel guests. Get your shuttle ticket and confirm the return sailing times with the concierge before leaving the hotel that morning. If the next boat would make you late, arrange a suitable taxi/Grab from the station area.
